A `Frame` cue points the slide's camera at any element by its id:
a word, a shape, even a frame on a hidden layer.
That covers a whole talk laid out on one canvas,
a diagram explained one part at a time,
or detail far too small to see from the back row.
Between two stops the camera pulls out just far enough to see both,
so a long move never turns into a blind pan.

The camera is the SVG viewBox, so nothing is ever rasterised.
The slide hiding in the dot of the i is about 4,500 times smaller than the title and still crisp.

Stepping backward flies the same path in reverse.
